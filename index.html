Core Capabilities

Everything you need to cruise your semester.

Auto-Marking Zones

Walk to your classroom and mark the coordinate once. When your class starts, background location matches the schedule and marks you present automatically. Coordinates are stored locally and never leave your device.

Real-time calculation tells you exactly how many lectures you can bunk without dropping below college thresholds. Automatically alerts you when a subject enters critical warning zones.

Answering Doubts

Frequently Asked Questions

Does AttendEase send my location coordinates to servers?

expand_more

No. Your privacy is our priority. Coordinates marked for classrooms are kept on-device inside a secure SQLite container. Location queries are completed strictly in local background tasks. Only attendance totals/profiles are shared with your squad if cloud backup is explicitly signed into.

How does the Bunk Budget system calculate safety?

expand_more

The system utilizes variables: your current present/total lectures, college minimum criteria (default 75%), and remaining semester calendar weeks. It maps out mathematical projection models to display the maximum potential bunks allowed without dropping below criteria threshold.

Can I change the minimum required attendance criteria?

expand_more

Yes. During the onboarding step, or at any point in Settings, you can configure your college's criteria using a stepper (ranges from 65% to 85%). The budget metrics update immediately across all panels.
